# Landing · Esencia & Grano

Scope: the whole single-page site (`src/routes/+page.svelte` and `src/lib/components/site/*`). Mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: home cooks, health-conscious shoppers and food businesses in Ibagué, mostly on phones; they need to know what the store has and order it. Action: send an order by WhatsApp. Proof on hand: +120 products (confirmed), home delivery and pickup, hours, location, payment. Imagery: AI-generated product still lifes, labeled illustrative until the owner's real photos replace them. Constraints: logo palette is the base (purple, magenta, gold, olive); fast on mid-range phones; WhatsApp never hidden; must not read as a template.

User steer (after two re-rolls): modern, product as protagonist, Apple and Nespresso as the craft bar.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The ingredient is the hero, lit like a product launch: macro still lifes on aubergine night under one warm key light, the page paced as cinematic chapters that all end in a WhatsApp order. It refuses the cream-and-kraft organic grocery and the row of equal category cards.

OWN-WORLD: Aubergine-night ground (#0d0612 to #1f0c2c), one gold key light (#e2a93b) as the only warm source and the primary action colour, magenta (#e0177d) only for live or selected state, ivory text (#f5efe8), quiet lilac-white chapters (#f6f2f8) for reading passages. Geist at display scale, tight tracking; pill controls; hairline ivory rules; images dissolve into the dark through radial masks, never boxed.

STORY: In seconds the visitor knows this is a bulk pantry in Ibagué with more than 120 products, believes it is fresh and sold in exact amounts, and builds a list that goes straight to WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed night. Headline "Lo justo. A granel." centred high at around 9vw; gold "Pide por WhatsApp" pill and "Recorre la despensa" link beneath; a macro composition of almonds, spices and grains rising from the bottom edge, floating ingredients at three parallax depths; a hairline meta strip (+120 productos · Lun a Sáb 8–20 · Domicilios). Translucent nav with a permanent WhatsApp pill.

FORM: user-pinned cinematic product launch (Apple × Nespresso), chosen over two rolled hands; seed:19247ef23e7005e7. Signature interaction: "Arma tu pedido", pick products and grams (100 g to 1 kg), live list, one tap sends the prefilled WhatsApp message. Motion grammar: scroll-linked chapters (scale, opacity, word-by-word light), parallax at 0.85/1/1.15, strong ease-out reveals, springy chips; reduced motion becomes fades.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
